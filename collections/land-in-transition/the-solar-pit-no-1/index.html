On 26 June 2026, the Sentinel-2 satellite looked down on Witznitz, in the Saxony region of eastern Germany. A dense grid of solar panels — one of Europe's largest photovoltaic parks — spreads across reclaimed land beside a turquoise former lignite-mining lake, with the raw exposed earth of an active pit still visible just beyond it. Old extraction and new generation, side by side.
