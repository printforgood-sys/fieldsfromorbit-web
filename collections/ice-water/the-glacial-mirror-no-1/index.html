The satellite passed over Torres del Paine National Park in Chilean Patagonia and caught a glacial lake between seasons: turquoise meltwater threaded with pale silt, framed by snow-dusted Andes peaks still holding onto autumn cloud. A near-black companion lake sits just beyond it, the two bodies of water reading like two different inks on the same page.
