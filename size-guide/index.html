Size & Format Guide

Available size

Every piece is offered at 16 × 20 in (40.6 × 50.8 cm) — a size that reads well on a hallway, office, or living-room wall without dominating the room. Additional sizes may be added as the catalogue grows.

Digital download

Every digital download is a full-resolution JPEG file, delivered by email within a minute or two of checkout — no watermark, no compression artifacts, ready to print.

File formatJPEG
Pixel dimensions4800 × 6000 px
Resolution300 DPI
Color profilesRGB
Typical file size4–7 MB
Native print size16 × 20 in (40.6 × 50.8 cm) at full 300 DPI

That's the exact pixel count for a crisp 16×20in print at 300 DPI. Print it yourself at home, at a local print shop, or through any online print service — at 16×20in, or scaled down proportionally for a smaller size (dropping below roughly 200 DPI starts to soften fine detail, so we wouldn't size it up past 16×20in).

Canvas print

Not offered yet — join the waitlist on any image page and we'll email you the moment canvas prints are available. Gallery-wrapped, ready to hang, no framing or assembly required.

Shipping

Canvas prints, once available, will be made to order and ship internationally to you — no domestic-only restriction. Buyer pays shipping; exact rates will be shown at checkout for your address.
